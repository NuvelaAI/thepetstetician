Quick answer

Mobile dog haircuts & styling from The Pet-Stetician means a breed-standard or custom cut, done one-on-one by Julia in a fully equipped grooming van at your home. Every style is tailored to your dog’s coat, lifestyle, and your preferences.

Breed-standard or your own style

Some pet parents want the classic breed look; others want something practical for Florida weather, or just plain cute. Tell Julia what you like, share a photo if you have one, and she’ll tailor the cut to your dog’s coat type and temperament.

Doodles, poodles, and curly coats

Curly and wavy coats keep growing and mat quickly in humidity, so they need regular haircuts. Doodles, poodles, bichons, shih tzus, schnauzers, and other low-shedding breeds usually do best on a steady schedule.

A note on shaving double coats

Double-coated breeds like goldens, shepherds, and huskies usually shouldn’t be shaved down, because their coat helps regulate temperature and protect their skin from the sun. For these dogs, de-shedding and a tidy trim are usually the better choice.

Haircuts & Styling FAQ

How often does my dog need a haircut?

Most dogs with growing coats, like doodles, poodles, and shih tzus, do well with a haircut every 4–8 weeks. Julia can recommend a schedule based on your dog’s coat and the length you like.

What should I tell the groomer before a haircut?

Your preferred length and style, any problem areas, and anything about your dog’s temperament, health, or sensitive spots. You can add notes when you book online.

Where do you offer mobile haircuts & styling?

Across Palm Beach, Martin, and St. Lucie Counties, Florida, including West Palm Beach, Jupiter, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Stuart, Port St. Lucie, and everywhere in between.
