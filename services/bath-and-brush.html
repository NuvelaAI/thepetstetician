Quick answer

A mobile bath & brush is a deep-clean bath, conditioning, and full brush-out, done in The Pet-Stetician’s grooming van parked right outside your home in Palm Beach, Martin, or St. Lucie County. It’s a great way to keep your dog fresh and comfortable between haircuts.

What a bath & brush is for

A bath & brush is about a clean, healthy coat rather than a new haircut. It’s a great fit for short-coated dogs, for dogs between haircuts, and for any pup who’s been rolling in something they shouldn’t have.

Who it’s great for

  • Short-coated breeds like Labs, beagles, and boxers
  • Dogs who are between full haircuts
  • Beach and pool dogs who need salt, chlorine, and sand washed out
  • Senior dogs who do best with a simpler, gentler visit

Pair it with

Combine a bath & brush with de-shedding, nail trimming, or ear cleaning for a more complete refresh. If your dog needs a new look too, book haircuts & styling instead.

Good to Know

Bath & Brush FAQ

How often should my dog have a professional bath?

Most dogs do well with a professional bath every 4–8 weeks, depending on coat and lifestyle. Dogs who swim or play outside a lot may need one more often.

Is a bath & brush the same as a full groom?

No. A bath & brush focuses on cleaning, conditioning, and brushing the coat. If your dog also needs a haircut or style, book haircuts & styling instead.

Where do you offer mobile bath & brush?

Across Palm Beach, Martin, and St. Lucie Counties, Florida, including West Palm Beach, Jupiter, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Stuart, Port St. Lucie, and everywhere in between.
