Quick answer

Puppy grooming at The Pet-Stetician is a gentle, patient introduction to bathing, brushing, and nail trims, done one-on-one in a mobile grooming van at your home. Grooming close to home, with no cages or barking dogs, helps puppies grow up comfortable with grooming.

Why first grooms matter

A puppy’s first grooming experiences shape how they feel about grooming for life. Positive, unhurried early visits help them get used to being handled, bathed, dried, and brushed, which makes every future groom easier for everyone.

Why mobile grooming is great for puppies

  • No car ride to a strange place
  • No cages and no waiting with other dogs
  • One groomer, one puppy, full attention
  • Familiar surroundings right outside home

Helping your puppy at home

Gently handle your puppy’s paws, ears, and face every day, and keep short brushing sessions positive with treats. These small habits make real grooms much easier.

Puppy Grooming FAQ

When should my puppy have their first groom?

Check with your vet about vaccines and timing, then book a gentle first groom. Starting early with short, positive visits helps puppies get comfortable with grooming.

What happens at a puppy’s first groom?

The focus is on making grooming a good experience: a gentle bath, brushing, and a nail trim as your puppy is comfortable, with plenty of patience along the way.

Where do you offer mobile puppy grooming?

Across Palm Beach, Martin, and St. Lucie Counties, Florida, including West Palm Beach, Jupiter, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Stuart, Port St. Lucie, and everywhere in between.
