Water dogs and humid days

Dogs that swim, ride on the boat, or play by the water can end up with damp coats that stay humid and start to tangle or smell. A thorough bath, dry, and brush-out resets the coat, and regular ear cleaning matters for dogs who spend a lot of time in the water.

Julia can help you choose a coat length and grooming schedule that work with your dog’s time on the water.

North Palm Beach Mobile Grooming FAQ

Why does ear cleaning matter for dogs that swim?

Moisture trapped in the ears can lead to buildup and irritation. Gentle, regular ear cleaning as part of a groom helps keep ears clean. If you notice redness, odor, or lots of scratching, check with your vet.
