Trail dogs and outdoor adventures

Dogs who hike the trails or explore the scrub and riverbanks come home with sand, burrs, and tangles. A thorough bath and brush-out gets everything out, and a neat trim around the feet keeps outdoor dogs comfortable.

After outdoor adventures, check your dog for ticks and anything stuck in the coat. If you notice irritated skin or anything unusual, check with your vet.
