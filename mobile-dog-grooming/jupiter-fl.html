Beach dogs need a little extra care

Sand, salt water, and sun are hard on a coat. Salt can dry out skin and fur, and sand works its way down to the skin and between the toes. If your dog loves the dog beach along A1A, a fresh bath and thorough brush-out every few weeks keeps their coat soft and their skin comfortable.

Rinsing your dog with fresh water after the beach helps between appointments. Julia can also suggest a grooming schedule and a coat length that’s easier to keep clean through the summer.
