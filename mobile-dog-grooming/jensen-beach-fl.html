Quick answer

Yes. The Pet-Stetician offers mobile dog grooming in Jensen Beach, FL. Julia brings a fully equipped grooming van to your home and grooms your dog one-on-one, with no cages, no long waits, and no car rides. Book online or call or text 973.896.4547.

Lagoon and beach life

Between lagoon walks, beach days, and Florida sun, Jensen Beach dogs get dirty in the best way. A deep-clean bath and conditioner help restore a coat dried out by salt and sun, and a brush-out removes the sand and loose undercoat that builds up.

Julia uses premium, pet-safe products and grooms one-on-one, so your dog gets a gentle, unhurried experience every time.
