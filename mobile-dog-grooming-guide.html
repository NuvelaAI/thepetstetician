How Mobile Dog Grooming Works

Everything you need to know about getting your dog groomed at home, from booking to the final brush-out.

🐾 Book Online 📞 Call 973.896.4547

Quick answer

Mobile dog grooming means the groomer comes to you. With The Pet-Stetician, you book online, Julia arrives at your home in a fully equipped grooming van, grooms your dog one-on-one right outside your door, and hands them back clean and happy. There’s no car ride, no cage, and no waiting.

How a mobile grooming appointment works

  1. Book. Book online through our booking page, or call or text 973.896.4547. Add your address, your dog’s breed, and any notes.
  2. The van arrives. Julia pulls up in the grooming van and parks close to your home.
  3. One-on-one groom. Your dog is groomed in the van with premium, pet-safe products, with no other dogs and no cages.
  4. Back home, looking great. Your dog comes back to you clean, happy, and looking their best.

Mobile grooming vs. a grooming salon

How to get ready for your appointment

  • Share gate codes, guest-list details, or parking rules in your booking notes.
  • Take your dog out for a potty break before the groom.
  • Mention any health issues, sensitive spots, or behavior concerns.
  • If you have a style in mind, have a photo ready to show Julia.

What affects the price?

Pricing depends on your dog’s size, coat type, and the services you choose, such as a bath & brush, haircut & styling, or de-shedding. Reach out for a quick, personalized quote.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well with grooming every 4–8 weeks, depending on breed and coat. Curly and doodle coats usually need the shorter end of that range; short coats can often go longer. Starting a puppy early makes every future groom easier.
